# DE-D0

**An AI-assisted content production system, from idea to publication.**

DE-D0 brings research, writing, video production, quality review, and publishing
into one coordinated workflow. Its focus is Polish-language YouTube content,
with a structure designed to support different channels and content formats.

The goal is to automate routine work while keeping quality, spending, and
publishing under the owner's control.

## How it works

1. **Research** gathers information, topic ideas, and performance data.
2. **Planning and writing** turn selected ideas into scripts.
3. **Production** creates narration, visuals, subtitles, and the finished video.
4. **Quality review** checks topics, scripts, and videos before they move forward.
5. **Publishing** manages approved videos and verifies publication on YouTube.
6. **Feedback** brings results back into research and future planning.

A central platform coordinates the process. Each department has a clear role,
and work moves between departments through shared communication rules.

## How the organization is structured

| Area | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | Coordinates workflows, schedules, and checks required to proceed. |
| Data | Collects facts, tracks results, and maintains useful knowledge. |
| Decision | Chooses topics and prepares scripts. |
| Production | Turns approved scripts into videos. |
| Quality Assurance | Independently evaluates work and returns a verdict. |
| Publication | Manages the video buffer, uploads, scheduling, and publication checks. |
| Context and Owner Panel | Maintains project guidance and gives the owner visibility and control. |
| Shared Contracts | Defines how departments exchange information. |
| Codex Control | Organizes development requests and proposals through GitHub Issues. |

These areas are maintained in separate repositories so they can evolve without
mixing responsibilities. Quality Assurance evaluates the work; the department
responsible for it makes any required corrections.

## Owner control

The system runs on a server and is supervised through a private owner panel.
The owner can monitor progress, review outcomes, and stop production.

Automation must respect quality checks, budget limits, and publishing rules.
The project is being developed incrementally, with an emphasis on traceable
decisions and reliable operation.
